Troubleshooting & FAQs

The images look almost black.

Colour is stored in linear light, not sRGB. Apply an sRGB transfer function before display or training. Do not apply an inverse sRGB; that crushes the data.

Depth has huge values around 65504.

That is the sky / at-infinity sentinel, not a distance. Mask it: valid = depth < 65504.0. Projection failure is a separate value, 0.0.

A W × W EXR looks vertically squashed when read as a panorama.

The file packs two eyes (left on top, right on bottom). Split it into halves, then treat each half as a W × W/2 ERP.

Depth resampled to another resolution shows surfaces that are not there.

Never bilinearly resample depth; use nearest-neighbour, so a discontinuity does not average into a surface that is not there.

Reflective surfaces (water, glass) look wrong or vary slice-to-slice.

Capture with Lumen hardware reflections; under screen-space reflection each 30° slice reflects a different view. The manifest's RenderParams column records which mode was used.

Two different alpha channels.

depth.A is a translucency mask; normal.A is a validity mask. They mean different things and do not coincide.

Release & Updates
